California Tax Withholding
CA Franchise Tax Board — Withholding Exemption Certificate (Form 590 equivalent)

California requires property management companies to withhold income tax from owner payments unless the owner certifies they are exempt. The information below determines your withholding status.

Federal law requires us to collect your taxpayer information so we can issue a Form 1099 at year end reporting the income paid to you. This is standard for all property owners we work with.
